## Technical Recording Guidelines
1. **Audio**: Cardioid USB microphone; normalize audio to -14 LUFS.
2. **Display Scaling**: Set Windows display scaling to 100% or 125% at 1920x1080 to ensure text remains crisp and legible.
3. **Cursor**: Smooth cursor movements; pause 1-2 seconds after opening modals before clicking.
4. **Browser**: Chrome or Edge in full screen (F11) with bookmarks bar hidden.
