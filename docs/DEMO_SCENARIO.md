## 1. The 60-Second Elevator Pitch (The Hook & Proof)

**Target Audience**: Judges, Investors, Marketing Agency Leads  
**Key Demonstration**: Proving that accumulated campaign experience demonstrably alters future marketing recommendations across completely separate application sessions.

### Step-by-Step Script (60 Seconds)

1. **[00:00 - 00:15] The Core Problem**:
   - *"Every marketing agency makes the same mistake: they generate campaigns in a vacuum. Generic AI tools produce the exact same clichés because they forget past results. Here is 'The Taco Syndicate', a college-town Mexican restaurant."*
2. **[00:15 - 00:30] Naive Generation vs Stored Memory**:
   - Open **Campaign Studio**. Show the brief: *"Plan a weekend promotion to drive student orders."*
   - Toggle **Memory Evolution**:
     - **Mode A (Zero Memory)**: Recommends *"10% off on Saturday afternoon with #TacoLovers"*. (Generic, ineffective, ignores student sleeping patterns).
     - **Mode B (Hindsight Memory)**: Instantly recommends *"After-9PM Flash BOGO with Student ID"*.
3. **[00:30 - 00:45] The Memory Citations**:
   - Click on the strategy's **Memory Evidence Chips**:
     - *"Look at the citations: CampaignEcho recalls Campaign #1 where late-night timing generated 380 redemptions, and reflects on Campaign #2 where a gourmet truffle experiment failed because students rejected premium pricing."*
4. **[00:45 - 01:00] The Retention Loop & Fresh Session Proof**:
   - Close the browser tab. Re-open a fresh session.
   - Show the **Memory Explorer**: The memory survived process termination in Vectorize Hindsight.
   - Submit new results: *"Every campaign teaches the next. CampaignEcho turns marketing history into an unfair strategic advantage."*

## 2. The 3-Minute Comprehensive Product Walkthrough

### Act 1: Multi-Brand Isolation (45s)
- Navigate to `/brands`.
- Show how an agency manages multiple brand profiles (`The Taco Syndicate`, `Luxe Parisian Spa`).
- Demonstrate that opening `Luxe Parisian Spa` and recalling memories returns zero taco or student promo records. Memory banks are strictly isolated 1:1.

### Act 2: Campaign History & Post-Mortem Feedback (45s)
- Navigate to `/campaign-history`.
- Inspect the 3 historical campaigns of *The Taco Syndicate*:
  1. *Late Night Burrito Drops* (High Reach, 380 redemptions, proven BOGO lever).
  2. *Gourmet Truffle Taco Tasting* (High spend, poor conversion, lesson: students reject upscale pretension).
  3. *Exam Week Energy Fuel* (Sunday night spike, high ROAS).
- Click into the drawer to inspect actual vs. target metrics and qualitative post-mortem notes.

### Act 3: Guided Campaign Studio & Strategy Generation (45s)
- Navigate to `/campaign-studio`.
- Enter brief: *"Plan an upcoming finals-week promotion for West Campus students."*
- Watch the live telemetry cards:
  - Blue chip: 3 Relevant memories recalled.
  - Violet chip: Hindsight reflection synthesized.
- Review the generated Strategy Deck:
  - 3 Multi-angle Reel concepts with viral hooks and visual treatments.
  - Memory citations under each concept.
  - Explicit KPI targets and execution assumptions.

### Act 4: The Continuous Feedback Loop (45s)
- Simulate campaign execution completion.
- Click **"Record Campaign Results"**.
- Input: `Spend: $450`, `Conversions: 410`, `Feedback: Outstanding late-night turnout`.
- Click **"Retain to Hindsight"**.
- Navigate to `/memory-explorer`. Show the new **Retain** node appear on the interactive timeline.
- Conclude: The next campaign brief will now automatically factor in this newly retained data point.
